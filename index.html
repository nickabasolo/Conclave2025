About this site

The 2025 conclave to elect a successor to Pope Francis began on May 7, 2025. Just 133 cardinal electors will choose the next leader of the Catholic Church — but who are they?

This site is a personal side project created to explore and visualize this unique group of electors. It's also been a fun way for me to practice front-end development and data visualization.

The information here is based on official Vatican consistory announcements, compiled via the Wikipedia page on the 2025 papal conclave. While I've done my best to ensure accuracy, this is not an official source of information.
